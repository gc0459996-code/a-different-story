What to Bring & Expect

  • A valid photo ID (driver license, state ID or passport) at every session.
  • Your court order or probation paperwork, if you have it, so your certificate has the right case details.
  • Arrive on time. Late arrivals and missed sessions can't be counted, and you may need to re-enroll.
  • Come sober. Anyone who appears impaired will be dismissed from class.
  • Take part in discussions and pass the course test with 70% or higher.
  • Phones silenced and put away during class.

Why the Deadline Matters

If you are placed on probation for a DWI, Texas law generally requires you to finish the program within 180 days. If you don't, DPS can suspend or revoke your driver license, and it stays suspended until you complete the class. Only the court that handled your case can grant an extension, so talk to your attorney or probation officer if you're short on time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which class do I need?

A first DWI conviction with probation usually requires the 12-hour DWI Education Program. A second or later DWI usually requires the 32-hour DWI Intervention Program. Your court order or probation officer will tell you which one. Call us if you're unsure.

Is this class approved by the State of Texas?

Yes. A Different Story is licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) to teach DWI Education and DWI Intervention. Our provider number is #_____.

Can I take the class before my court date?

Yes. Many people complete DWI Education before going to court. Check with your attorney first to make sure it fits your case.

Will this class get my suspended license back?

Completing the DWI Education Program is what DPS requires to reinstate a license suspended for not finishing it. You may also need to pay a reinstatement fee to DPS. If your license is suspended now, ask the court about an occupational license so you can drive legally in the meantime.

Are classes in person or online?

In person at our Waco location / live online / both.

What if I miss a session or show up late?

State rules require you to complete every hour. If you miss a session or arrive late, you may need to make it up or re-enroll in a later class. Make-up and transfer fee policy.

How do I get my certificate?

When you finish the class and pass the course test, we issue your state certificate of completion. We can also send a copy to your probation officer or attorney. Just tell us who when you enroll.

Is the Victim Impact Panel included?

No. The Victim Impact Panel (VIP) is a separate requirement that some courts order. We offer it / we can refer you.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. Once you enroll in a course, you agree that you are not eligible for a refund except under the guarantee below.

30-day money back guarantee. If you sign up for the correct course, complete it, receive your certificate, and submit it on time, but the court or probation officer does not accept your A Different Story certificate, let us know with evidence within 30 days. We will first try to work it out with the court or probation officer, and if they still don't accept the certificate, we will issue a full refund.
